## 8. Launch-day checklist (when 12+ testers are in)

1. Play Console → Create app (name DoingTheDoings, default language Spanish, Free).
2. Upload `.aab` to **Closed testing** track → create email list with tester Gmails.
3. Fill **Store listing** (this file), **Data safety** (§4), **Content rating** (§5),
   **App access** (§6), target audience 18+.
4. Roll out the closed test → send testers the opt-in link → the **14-day clock starts**.
5. After 14 continuous days with 12+ opted-in testers → **Apply for production** →
   answer the questionnaire (what you tested, feedback received, what you fixed).
6. After approval → move the release to **Production**. 🎉
